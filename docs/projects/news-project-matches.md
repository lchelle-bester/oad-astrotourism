# Which news posts belong to which project

Suggested matches for tagging the 27 "OAD projects" news posts in the Studio (10 Nov 2026). These are suggestions for you to check, not decisions. Nothing has been tagged yet.

## How I matched them

I read the text of every post and compared it with the summary of every one of the 20 projects, including how much wording they share. Three levels of confidence:

- **Certain**: the post copies the project's own description, or names the project in its title or first lines.
- **High**: the same place and organisation, and only one project fits.
- **Medium**: it fits from context (same organisation or year), but a neighbouring project could also fit. Worth a second look.

Out of 27 posts: **22 have a suggested project** (10 certain, 6 high, 6 medium), **1 matches none** and **4 are unsure**.

## To tag one

Open the post in the Studio, check the category is "OAD projects", then use **Projects this post is about** to add the project, and publish. The posts are in the order the Studio lists them (newest first).

## Three things to decide (if unsure draft email to joy)

**The two Tanzania projects.** "Astro-tourism development in Tanzania" (2022) and "Astro Tourism Development Tanzania" (2023) have exactly the same summary text, word for word. So post 21 fits both equally. I suggested the 2022 one because the post is dated December 2021, like the other overviews for 2022 projects. Joy can say whether the 2023 one is a follow-on that should share it.

**The four Astrostays posts (15, 16, 22, 23).** No project summary mentions Astrostays. They are probably related to "Astronomy for Himalayan Livelihood Creation": post 23 says the Phyang Monastery one was set up by "OAD's implementation partner, the Global Himalayan Expedition", the same partner as that project, and post 15 describes a 2019 pilot in Ladakh. But a post can only say what its text says, and "probably" is not enough to put it on a project's page. I would leave these four untagged until Joy confirms.

**The AstroTribe posts (5, 7, 10, 14).** I first thought AstroTribe was a separate project from the directory. It isn't: Phase 2's summary names AstroTribe, and the 2022 workshop is the same programme. The two are sequential batches of the same work, so a post could reasonably go on either or both. Each of these four shows the one I think fits best.
